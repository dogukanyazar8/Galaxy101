# Third-party assets

## Physical tile contact audio candidate — not included in the game

An audition candidate was derived from **Domino pieces 2** by **Macif**, published2015-11-21 on [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/Macif/sounds/329100/). Licensed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); commercial use and modification permitted. The public HQ preview was used. Trimming, mono downmix, filtering, fades and level changes were applied. Source, checksum and regeneration instructions: [source-assets/audio/README.md](source-assets/audio/README.md). The user rejected this candidate; it remains an audition artifact and has not replaced game audio.

Other table geometry, numeral atlas, procedural textures and color markers remain authored in this project. Unity/package third-party notices shipped by their respective dependencies remain applicable.

Montserrat, Baloo 2 and Nunito are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Font files and their license copies are in `Assets/Galaxy101/Resources/Galaxy101/Fonts/`. Nunito weight 900 was instantiated from the official Google Fonts variable font for the supplied HTML sale badge.
